PUF!

Balloon puzzle game by Novera Studio · Android · com.puf.game

About the game

PUF! is a colourful puzzle game. Blow air from one balloon into its neighbour; when three full balloons of the same colour line up, they pop. Three ways to play — Hava Ver (blow air), Hava Yolu (join same colours) and Dokun Patlat (tap a group) — across hundreds of levels with special balloons, obstacles, boosters and a toy to rescue.
