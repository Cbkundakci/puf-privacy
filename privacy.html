This Privacy Policy explains how Novera Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile game PUF! (package com.puf.game).

1. Information we collect

  • Game data on your device — level progress, stars, coins, boosters, daily-gift state and settings (sound, music volume, vibration). It is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Advertising identifier — the game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions in order to show and measure ads.
  • Analytics & crash reports — we may use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how levels are played (for example level start, win or loss) and to fix crashes. This includes app events, device/app information and crash logs. It is not used to identify you personally.

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location permission or any account sign-up.

2. How we use information

  • Run the game and keep your progress on your device
  • Show optional rewarded ads and measure their performance
  • Balance level difficulty and fix bugs and crashes

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

These Google services process data under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase

4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and similar regions)

Where required by law, the game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalised ads and related processing. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options in the game, or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

5. Data retention & deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. We do not keep a server-side account for you. Advertising and analytics data are kept by Google according to its retention policies. See Data Deletion for how to remove your data. All data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit.

6. Children

PUF! is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing. Contact us at the address below.
